Compose Session Viewer connects to a compose-preview serve session and presents the rendered Compose UI as if it were a complete app.

Tap a session link (or pick a server discovered on your network) and the app streams live frames from the server, painting each update as it arrives. Touches, drags and key presses are forwarded back into the running composition, so the remote preview behaves like a real, interactive app on your phone.

• Open straight from a composeai:// link or a serve viewer URL
• Discover preview servers on your LAN via mDNS — no typing IP addresses
• Live frame streaming over a WebSocket lane
• Forward taps, drags and keys back to the composition
• Plaintext on the trusted LAN; the session token in the link is the gate

This is a companion client for the compose-ai-tools "serve" feature. It is aimed at developers and designers reviewing Compose UI on real devices.
